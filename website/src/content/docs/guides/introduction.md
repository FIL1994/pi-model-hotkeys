Pi Model Hotkeys gives you **nine configurable presets** for switching models in Pi. Press <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>1</kbd> through <kbd>9</kbd> to jump straight to a favorite setup, without opening a picker.

## A small extension for a smoother workflow

- **Go direct.** Every assigned slot points to a specific provider and model.
- **Save a thinking level.** Use the same model in multiple slots with different thinking settings.
- **Keep your context.** Switching preserves your conversation and leaves Pi's default model for new sessions unchanged.
- **See your shortcuts.** A compact legend below the editor highlights matching presets.
- **Take them with you.** Assignments persist across projects and sessions.

## The basic loop

1. [Install the extension](../installation/).
2. Run `/model-hotkeys` and assign a slot.
3. Press the slot's shortcut whenever Pi is idle.

You can customize labels, display names, and the shortcut modifier. Optional Alt+wheel switching is available in fullscreen mode.

:::tip[Start with what already works]
Choose **Use current model and thinking** to turn your current setup into your first preset.
:::
